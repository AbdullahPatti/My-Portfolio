ACM TALLIP · SubmittedFirst author

Domain Robustness of Multilingual NLP Models Across Urdu and Roman Urdu Scripts

Muhammad Abdullah Haroon, Maryam Bashir

Fine-tuned encoders break under script and domain shift; few-shot LLMs hold up better.

26datasets released
188,309examples
83–100%target-trained wins
Details
  • Released 26 datasets (188,309 examples) and 7 transfer matrices over sentiment, hate speech, cyber abuse, fake news, QA validation and 3-class polarity, with documented provenance and licensing.
  • A target-trained model wins 83–100% of shifts, proving encoder losses are true robustness failures; fake news does not transfer for either model family.
GitHubDatasetZenodo
TMLR · SubmittedFirst author

Does Compression Preserve Adversarial Patch Robustness?

Muhammad Abdullah Haroon, A. R. Antall, A. Hadi, D. Farhat

Patch robustness follows lost capacity, not sparsity: pruning keeps it, shrinking breaks it.

13ResNet-18 models
61.2→52.9%dense patched acc.
3seeds each
Details
  • First joint test of pruning, quantization and distillation against patch attacks: a 13-model ResNet-18 zoo at 2/4/8× compression, universal and per-image LaVAN attacks, 3 seeds.
  • Pruned models held baseline robustness, while equally sized dense models fell from 61.2% to 52.9% patched accuracy.
  • Certified with PatchCleanser (60.6 points via mask augmentation); adaptive attacks ruled out gradient masking.
GitHubLive demo
arXiv:2607.14131 · Jul 2026Sole author

When Benchmarks Mislead: Shortcut Learning in Multilingual Fake News and Sarcasm Detection

Muhammad Abdullah Haroon

Urdu fake-news classifiers learn article length, not content.

3.4×longer fake articles
0.771→0.005transfer macro-F1
3tasks tested
Details
  • Fake Urdu articles run 3.4× longer than real ones, so classifiers learn length instead of content, sinking transfer to 0.005 macro-F1.
  • Compared XLM-RoBERTa with TF-IDF + Logistic Regression and SVM baselines; the pattern repeats in English fake news and sarcasm.
